# Brand Spec

## Reference

User-provided visual reference: an editorial restaurant menu poster with forest green, cream, organic circular forms, oversized display typography, and alternating food-image focal points. Use it as an inspiration reference only; do not reproduce its exact copy, logo, contact details, or artwork.

## Applied system

- World: forest green + cream + muted lime.
- Hero object: one dish or drink at a time, staged inside a circular/organic frame.
- Guest action: explore the menu, then add an item to the persistent cart.
- Staff action: publish menu changes safely and see their edge/realtime status.
